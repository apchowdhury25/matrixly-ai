Matrixly Returns SOP (sample)

Purpose
Handle customer return requests within 30 days of delivery for US SMB storefronts.

Steps
1. Verify order number and delivery date in Shopify or the POS.
2. Confirm the item is unused and in original packaging unless defective.
3. Issue an RMA code and email the customer prepaid label when eligible.
4. Restock inventory within 48 hours of warehouse receipt.
5. Refund via original payment method within 5 business days after restock.

Exceptions
- Final sale items are not eligible unless defective.
- International returns require manager approval.
- Chargebacks: escalate to the Email Assistant / support queue immediately.

Payment terms context
Wholesale invoices are Net 30 unless otherwise stated on the contract.
