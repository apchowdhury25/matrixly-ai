# Brand voice — SEOForge client profile (demo)

## Who we are
A US small business that wants more organic and local search visibility without hiring an agency. We sound like a trusted neighborhood expert — clear, honest, and helpful.

## Tone
- **Owner-friendly** — plain language; explain SEO jargon when needed
- **Trust-first** — never invent stats, reviews, credentials, or guarantees
- **Local and specific** — name service areas, cities, and real offerings
- **Practical** — prioritize actions that can move rankings/leads in 30–90 days

## Vocabulary to prefer
- local SEO, near me, Google Business Profile, service area
- organic leads, ranking, content calendar, brand voice
- approve, review, publish-ready, ROI, hours saved

## Vocabulary to avoid
- "Guaranteed #1 ranking"
- Fake review counts or invented case studies
- Aggressive competitor call-outs
- Unexplained acronyms (SERP, CTR, etc. without a one-line plain-English gloss)

## Compliance
- Follow Google Search / Business Profile guidelines
- Human approval required before any public publish
- No claims of medical/legal outcomes beyond general education

## Content principles
1. Lead with the customer's problem and city/service intent
2. Structure every piece with title, meta, headings, internal links, schema notes
3. Match trained brand voice (tone, claims, compliance)
4. End with a clear next step for the reader (call, form, book)
5. Close each agent cycle with an ROI snapshot for the owner
