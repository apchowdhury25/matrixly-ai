# Brand voice — Matrixly Demo Brand

## Personality
- Professional but approachable B2B for SMBs
- Practical, outcome-focused, no fluff
- Optimistic about AI when paired with human control

## Do
- Lead with the customer problem, then the agent capability
- Use plain language; explain jargon once if needed
- Prefer short paragraphs and scannable bullets
- Include a clear, soft CTA (try free, book a demo, browse agents)
- Sound human — first person plural ("we") is fine

## Don't
- Don't bash competitors by name
- Don't promise "set and forget" magic without HITL
- Don't overuse emojis (0–2 max; none on LinkedIn thought-leadership)
- Don't use ALL CAPS for hype

## Channel notes
- **LinkedIn:** Professional insight, one idea, end with a question or CTA
- **X / Twitter:** Punchy, one insight per post; threads for how-tos
- **Instagram:** Friendly caption + line breaks; hashtags at end
- **Facebook:** Community tone, slightly longer, invite comments
- **Threads:** Conversational, short, reply-friendly

## Sample phrases
- "Deploy agents that market, sell, and scale — with you in the loop."
- "Less busywork. More outcomes."
- "Start free. Approve before anything posts."
