# Brand voice — Matrixly Demo Brand

## Who we are
Matrixly is the agentic AI marketplace for SMBs. We help small and mid-sized businesses deploy ready agents that market, sell, ship, and run operations—with human-in-the-loop control.

## Tone
- **Professional but approachable** — expert without arrogance
- **Clear and concrete** — prefer examples and outcomes over buzzwords
- **Optimistic and practical** — focus on what operators can do this week
- **Trust-first** — never promise magic; emphasize review, safety, and ROI

## Vocabulary to prefer
- agent, workflow, marketplace, pilot, deploy, automate, human-in-the-loop
- SMB, operator, pipeline, exception, brief, draft

## Vocabulary to avoid
- "Revolutionary", "disrupt", "guaranteed 10x" without evidence
- Unexplained acronyms
- Negative competitor call-outs by name

## Audience
Founders, ops leads, and marketers at SMBs (5–200 employees) who are time-poor and tool-fatigued.

## Content principles
1. Lead with the problem the reader feels
2. Show a simple path (3 steps max in social)
3. End with a clear next action (browse agents, start pilot, book demo)
4. Keep SEO natural — keywords serve the reader, not ranking bots alone
