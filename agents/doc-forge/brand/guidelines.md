# Brand & document guidelines — Matrixly Demo Co

## Voice
- Professional, clear, practical
- Confident without hype
- Client-centric: outcomes over features
- Never invent legal terms beyond the approved legal block

## Document structure
1. Cover / title + client + date
2. Executive summary (3–5 sentences)
3. Scope / deliverables
4. Pricing table (use catalog SKUs when possible)
5. Timeline
6. Assumptions & exclusions
7. Legal / terms (from approved legal language)
8. Next steps / signature block

## Pricing rules
- Currency: USD
- Do not exceed configured max discount without a note for human review
- Always show payment terms (default Net 30)
- Pilot packages should mention human-in-the-loop where agents are sold

## Visual
- Primary: #117ACA
- Prefer clean markdown/HTML suitable for PDF export
- Footer: Confidential — prepared by Matrixly Demo Co

## Do not
- Promise SLAs not in the template
- Use competitor names
- Leave placeholder tokens like {{client}} in final drafts
