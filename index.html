Example flow · shipping

From order to doorstep — without the chaos

Matrixly agents run fulfillment end to end so you ship faster, spend less on carriers, and answer fewer “where’s my order?” messages.

  1. 1

    Order comes in

    Shopify, WooCommerce, or POS orders enter the queue automatically.

  2. 2

    Stock check

    Confirms inventory, splits warehouses, and flags backorders early.

  3. 3

    Best rate

    Compares UPS, FedEx, USPS, and regional options for cost vs. speed.

  4. 4

    Label & pack

    Prints labels, pack slips, and pick lists for the warehouse team.

  5. 5

    Keep customers informed

    SMS and email on ship, delay, and out-for-delivery — before they ask.

  6. 6

    Fix problems

    Retries failed scans, opens claims, and only escalates when a human is needed.
