Operating system

From discovery to managed workforce

  1. 1

    Discovery — one highest-pain workflow

    Low-pressure fit call. We pick the single workflow where multi-step automation will show hours and dollars fastest. We set your accepted hourly rate for the value ledger.

  2. 2

    Setup (usually 3–7 days)

    Build and connect the digital employee on Matrixly’s stack (persistent agents, shared sheets, email/CRM tools). Create the shared coaching channel. Write outputs to shared files the whole team can see.

  3. 3

    On-site visit & intensive coaching (weeks 1–2)

    Founder/CEO visits (or hybrid). Live training with your team. We stay heavy in the shared channel so you learn by watching how we brief, correct, and approve the agent.

  4. 4

    Ongoing management + Monday ROI

    Light monitoring, fix-before-you-notice reliability, and a weekly value report every Monday. When the first agent is clearly saving time, we propose a second specialized digital employee.

Retention engine

The weekly ROI report

This is the single strongest reason clients keep paying. A clear “you saved $X this week” message re-sells the retainer every seven days.

Core metrics every week

  • 1Hours saved — task counts × realistic minutes per task (e.g. 8–15 min per invoice)
  • 2Dollar value — hours × your fully-loaded hourly rate (we ask during onboarding; often $75–$125 for owner time)
  • 3Tasks completed — invoices, leads, posts, files — visible volume even when $ fluctuates
  • 4Business outcomes — $ processed, calls booked, response time, content published
  • 5Uptime / reliability — “99.4% available · 0 critical failures”
  • 6Escalations fixed — times we intervened, and how fast — proof of management

Conservative estimates only. Cumulative total since day one: “187 hours / $18,700 value.”

Common questions

Is $499 setup or monthly?

Founding White Glove is a $499/month managed retainer. One-time setup is scoped per workflow (typically $499–$1,500 for early clients). Growth and Operations suites use higher setup + retainer as complexity grows.

What’s the real product — the agent or the coaching?

Coaching and ongoing management. The agent is the digital employee; the product is that it keeps working, you understand it, and you see weekly ROI without becoming a prompt engineer.

Who visits on-site?

Initially the founder/CEO. As capacity grows, trained White Glove coaches deliver under the same standards. You’ll always have a named partner.

How is this different from DIY agents that break?

Reliability is a selling point. We manage uptime, write to shared sheets (not private notes), stay in your channel, and fix issues before they become your Monday fire drill.

Can we start on Scale and upgrade?

Yes. Many owners explore self-serve, then move to White Glove when they want a managed digital employee and weekly value reporting. We’ll map credit from active platform use into the conversation.
