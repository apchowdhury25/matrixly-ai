Connect your Gmail

Connect your Gmail so your AI assistant can keep your inbox under control while you run the business.

Privacy: Your emails never leave your control. We do not train on them. You can revoke access any time in your Google account.

What your Email Assistant will do

  • Sort and label mail (Urgent, Needs Reply, FYI, and more)
  • Draft replies for you to review — never auto-sends to customers
  • Flag time-sensitive items so nothing slips
  • Email you a short daily brief of what matters

Google permissions (plain English)

  • Read your mail so it can triage the inbox
  • Create labels & drafts so work is organized for you
  • Send only the daily brief to you (not customers)

Almost there — 2 minutes

Gmail connects securely on your computer through the Email Assistant. Paste these commands in a terminal (or ask your setup partner). No coding required beyond copy-paste.

Drafts only for customer replies. Daily brief emails only go to you.

1. One-time Google setup

Follow the copy-paste guide: agents/email-assistant/scripts/setup_oauth.md

2. Connect

cd agents/email-assistant python -m venv .venv .\.venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1 pip install -r requirements.txt python -m src.cli connect-gmail

Your browser opens → sign in → Allow. You should see “Success — Gmail connected”.

Open Email Assistant page

Hostinger / Outlook / IMAP

Not on Gmail? You can still use Email Assistant with the same inbox you use in Thunderbird or Outlook.

# In agents/email-assistant/.env EMAIL_BACKEND=imap EMAIL_PROFILE=hostinger EMAIL_HOSTINGER_USER=you@yourdomain.com EMAIL_HOSTINGER_PASSWORD=******** python -m src.cli auth python -m src.cli triage

Full steps are in the Email Assistant README under “Hostinger / IMAP”.

Open Email Assistant

Test Mode — sample inbox

See the agent work before connecting real email. No Google login needed.

Sample triage preview
• URGENT — AC unit not cooling (customer waiting)

• URGENT — Invoice past due

cd agents/email-assistant python -m src.cli test-mode python -m src.cli impact --test
✓

Gmail connected

Your Email Assistant is ready. Open it to triage, draft replies, and get your first 24-hour impact report.

Remember: replies stay as drafts until you send. Revoke anytime at Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
